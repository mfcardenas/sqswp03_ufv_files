## 🏨 Case Study: TravelEase

### System Description

**TravelEase** is a digital platform for booking tourism experiences that connects travelers with tourism service providers (hotels, tours, activities, restaurants, transportation).

**Main features:**
- 🔍 Advanced search for destinations and experiences
- 📅 Booking of personalized tourism packages
- 💳 Secure online payment
- 📱 Available on web (desktop and mobile) and native app
- 🗺️ Interactive guides with geolocation
- ⭐ Review and recommendation system
- 🌍 Multilanguage support
- 📧 Itinerary and travel document management

**Competitive context:**
TravelEase competes with platforms like Booking.com, Airbnb Experiences, GetYourGuide, Viator, and traditional travel agencies.

**Differentiator:**
Focus on personalized and curated experiences, not just transactional bookings. Aims to be the "Netflix of tourism" - recommends experiences based on traveler preferences.

#### Phase 1: Context of Use Analysis (30 min)

**Objective:** Identify and document the context of use of TravelEase according to ISO 9241-11

##### Activity 1.1: Identify User Profiles (10 min)

Brainstorm as a team and list **all types of users** who could use TravelEase.

**Guiding questions:**
- What ages do potential users have?
- What levels of technological experience do they have?
- What motivations do they have to travel? (pleasure, business, adventure, relaxation, culture)
- Do they travel alone, as a couple, with family, in a group?
- What economic capacity do they have?
- Do they have special needs? (disabilities, dietary restrictions, etc.)

**Deliverable:** List of at least **5-7 different profiles** with brief description of each one.

**Profile example:**
```
Profile: Young backpacker tourist
- Age: 18-28 years
- Technological competence: High (digital natives)
- Motivation: Adventure, culture discovery, low budget
- Devices: Mainly smartphone
- Context of use: On the move, variable connection
```

##### Activity 1.2: Define Main Tasks (10 min)

Select **2-3 main profiles** (the most important/frequent).

For each profile, identify **5-8 tasks** they would perform in TravelEase.

**Classify each task by:**
- **Frequency:** How often do they perform it? (once, occasional, frequent)
- **Complexity:** How complex is it? (simple, medium, complex)
- **Criticality:** How important is it for the user? (high, medium, low)

**Task example:**
```
Profile: Young backpacker tourist
Task: Search for cheap hostels in a city
- Frequency: Occasional (each time they plan a trip)
- Complexity: Medium (requires filtering by price, location, reviews)
- Criticality: High (essential for their type of trip)
```

**Deliverable:** Table of tasks by profile (use provided Excel template)

##### Activity 1.3: Analyze Equipment and Environments (10 min)

For each main profile, analyze:

**EQUIPMENT (Devices and software):**
- What devices do they use? (smartphone, tablet, laptop, desktop, kiosk)
- What operating system? (iOS, Android, Windows, macOS)
- What browsers? (Chrome, Safari, Firefox)
- What screen size?
- What storage/processing capacity?

**ENVIRONMENTS:**

**1. Physical Environment:**
- Where do they use the system? (home, office, airport, hotel, tourist destination)
- What lighting conditions? (natural light, artificial, variable)
- What noise level?
- Are they sitting, standing, moving?

**2. Technical Environment:**
- What type of connectivity do they have? (stable WiFi, 4G/5G mobile data, intermittent connection)
- Available bandwidth?
- Firewall/security restrictions?

**3. Social Environment:**
- Do they use the system alone or accompanied?
- Are there frequent interruptions?
- Do they collaborate with other users?

**4. Cultural Environment:**
- What language(s) do they speak?
- What level of familiarity with digital technology does their culture have?
- What cultural conventions affect their use? (date formats, currency, etc.)

**Deliverable:** Complete context analysis in template

#### Phase 3: Usability Metrics Definition (20 min)

**Objective:** Define specific metrics for effectiveness, efficiency and satisfaction for each persona

##### Activity 3.1: Review ISO 9241-11 Concepts (5 min)

Quickly review the definitions (from your preparatory readings):

**EFFECTIVENESS:**
- Definition: Accuracy and completeness with which users achieve specified objectives
- What does it measure?: Whether the user **achieves** what they want to do
- Metric examples: % of tasks completed successfully, % of errors, completion rate

**EFFICIENCY:**
- Definition: Resources expended in relation to the accuracy and completeness achieved
- What does it measure?: **How much effort/time** is required to achieve the objective
- Metric examples: Completion time, number of clicks/steps, cognitive load

**SATISFACTION:**
- Definition: Extent to which the user's needs and expectations are satisfied
- What does it measure?: **How happy** the user is with the experience
- Metric examples: SUS score, NPS, post-use rating, qualitative comments

##### Activity 3.2: Define Metrics by Persona (12 min)

For each of the 3 personas, define specific metrics of each type.

**Metrics must be:**
- ✅ **Specific:** Not vague (e.g., "good experience" ❌ → "SUS > 80" ✅)
- ✅ **Measurable:** With numbers or clear scales
- ✅ **Relevant:** Appropriate for that user profile
- ✅ **Realistic:** Possible to obtain in a real test

**Example of metrics for a persona:**

| Persona | Effectiveness Metric | Efficiency Metric | Satisfaction Metric |
|---------|---------------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **Laura García** (Young backpacker tourist, 24 years) | • 95% of searches result in at least 1 viable option<br>• 90% of bookings completed without errors<br>• 0 bookings cancelled due to system confusion | • Average search to booking time: < 5 min<br>• Maximum 15 clicks to complete booking<br>• 0 back steps due to navigation error | • System Usability Scale (SUS): > 75<br>• Likelihood to Recommend: > 8/10<br>• "I feel safe making the booking": > 4/5 |

**Tips:**
- Define **2-3 metrics** for each component (effectiveness, efficiency, satisfaction)
- Think about how you would **actually measure** this in a usability test
- Consider each persona's **priorities** (e.g., budget tourist prioritizes price over speed)

|--------------------------|----------------------|-------------------|----------------------|---------------|
| Laura García (Young tourist, 24) | ... | ... | ... | These metrics reflect that Laura prioritizes speed and ease of use since she searches in mobile contexts with little time... |

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Do I have to read the ENTIRE ISO 9241-11 standard?**  
A: No. Only the indicated sections (Intro, Terms, Context of use, Usability measures). Approx. 15-20 pages.

**Q: Can I use another AI besides ChatGPT?**  
A: Yes. Claude, Gemini, Copilot, or any other you prefer. The prompts are designed to work with any.

**Q: What do I do if the AI gives me a bad/generic response?**  
A: Iterate. Ask for more details, give additional context, reformulate the prompt. See `prompts_ia.md` for iteration examples.

**Q: Can I make up personas instead of using AI?**  
A: No. Part of this lab's learning is using generative AI effectively. You must document the process with AI.

**Q: Do the 3 personas have to be very different?**  
A: Yes. Don't make 3 variations of the same profile (e.g., 3 young tourists). Seek diversity: ages, motivations, technological competencies, contexts of use.

**Q: Can I use real people (friends, family)?**  
A: You can be inspired by real people, but personas must be REPRESENTATIVE of a user segment, not biographical of a specific individual.

**Q: How many metrics should I define per persona?**  
A: Minimum 2-3 for each component (effectiveness, efficiency, satisfaction). Total: 6-9 metrics per persona.

**Q: Do metrics have to be exact numbers?**  
A: Yes, they must be specific and measurable. Vague example ❌: "good experience". Specific ✅: "SUS > 80/100" or "booking time < 5 min".

**Q: Can I work alone instead of in a team?**  
A: Consult with the instructor. Generally teamwork is required to develop collaborative skills.

**Q: What if my team can't agree?**  
A: Debate is good. If disagreement persists, document both perspectives and justify. The instructor will value reasoning, not "single correct answer".

**Q: How long should it take to write the final report?**  
A: Approximately 3-4 hours if you did the work well in the in-person session. If you leave everything for later, much more.

**Q: Can I use images/photos in personas?**  
A: Yes, optional. You can use free image banks (Unsplash, Pexels) or AI face generators (Generated Photos). Cite the source.

**Q: What reference format do I use?**  
A: APA or IEEE, whichever you prefer. Be consistent.

**Q: Can I exceed 12 pages?**  
A: Preferably not. If you have a lot of content, use annexes. Synthesis is an important skill.

**Q: Is there a penalty for late submission?**
